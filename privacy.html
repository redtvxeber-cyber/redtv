Privacy Policy

RedTV Publisher · Last updated: November 6, 2026

1. What this tool is

RedTV Publisher is an internal tool operated by RED TV for publishing its own news videos to its own TikTok account. It is not a public service.

2. Information we access

When the account owner connects TikTok through TikTok Login Kit, the tool receives:

  • the TikTok account identifier (open ID), display name and username;
  • access and refresh tokens, used only to upload and publish videos on the owner's behalf.

The tool also handles the videos it creates and the captions (descriptions and hashtags) the owner approves.

3. How we use it

This information is used only to publish the owner's approved videos to the owner's TikTok account and to show the connected account name inside the tool. We do not use it for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

4. Storage and retention

Tokens and account details are stored locally on the server or computer that runs the tool. Temporary copies of videos are deleted after publishing, when the owner cancels, or automatically within 24 hours. Account details and tokens are kept until the owner disconnects the account or deletes them.

5. Sharing

We do not sell or share TikTok data with third parties. Video captions may be drafted with an AI text service (Google Gemini) from the video's title and narration text; no TikTok account data or tokens are sent to it.

6. Revoking access and deletion

The account owner can revoke the tool's access at any time in TikTok under Settings → Security and permissions. To have stored data deleted, contact us at the address below.
